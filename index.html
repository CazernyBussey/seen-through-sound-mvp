Speak now starts one request. A short tone means listening has started; speak after the tone. There is no ending tone. After you finish, it opens automatically. Select Cancel listening to cancel. Speak now is ready again afterward. Say Pause, Resume, Stop, Take me to ETIB Facebook, or Play Seen Through Sound. Each new request replaces the previous result.

If your browser blocks automatic playback, select Play once. Radio Podcast and Experience requests play the newest full episode here. Say Play it to resume the selected episode. Seen Through Sound continues through its published messages automatically; Pause and Resume keep your place, and Stop ends the playlist.

For microphone access, open this link directly in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android and allow the microphone. If voice recognition is unavailable, open the existing ETIB voice assistant.

If Speak now cannot hear you, dictate your request into this field using your keyboard’s Dictate button, then select Send request.

Voice requests are processed by ElevenLabs using the connected ETIB account. Recording is disabled, and conversation retention is configured to zero days. Your player session clears when you leave.
